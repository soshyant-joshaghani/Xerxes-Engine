## Pitch

Walk through the legend of Cyrus's birth: the king's dream, the order, the shepherd who could not obey it, the boy who played king. Your choices shape how the tale is told and which ending it reaches.

## Why it is this mode

Authored forward progress, no failure: Narrative.

## Rules (the `Sim`)

- **State:** Story variables, the current chapter.
- **Inputs:** Walk, look, talk, choose.
- **A round ends when:** An ending.
- **Result:** An ending (a branch, not a rank).

## Controls

| Keyboard / mouse | Touch |
|---|---|
| WASD + mouse, 1-3 choose | Tap to walk and talk |

## Offline, online, spectate

- **Offline:** You.
- **Online:** Not needed.
- **Spectate:** Watch the choices.

- Cinematic sequences and a timeline
- Dialogue staged in 3D
