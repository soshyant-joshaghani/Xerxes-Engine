Solo one player, one run
Time trial / high score
Neon White · speedrun boards · aim trainers
Ranked against your own and others' personal bests — no live opponent.
Challenge clear
Boss rush · escape room · puzzle gauntlet
Not numerically ranked — tiered pass/fail (bronze/silver/gold) instead.
Structurally empty
A "role" only means something relative to other players. Nothing to be distinct from.
Endurance run
Vampire Survivors · Risk of Rain · solo COD Zombies
No opponents needed — you're surviving the game itself. Ranked by time or wave survived.
Coop 2+ players, one side, shared fate
Shared high score
Horde survival · loot run · combo-synergy scoring
Bonus for plays that require both players together — not two solos in parallel.
Shared clear
Raid boss · heist run · timed dungeon clear
Succeed or fail as one unit. Optionally ranked by clear time.
Functional split
Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes · tank/support/dps encounter · hacker+guard heist
Each player sees different information and does a different job — neither can solve it alone. Cleaner proof than any combat example: zero shooting required.
Team endurance
Horde mode · zombie survival · tower defense
Whole team survives or wipes together — ranked by wave reached or time held.
Duel 2 sides (1 or many-a-side)
Head-to-head score
Football-style scoring · Halo skull-carry · king-of-hill points
Score usually comes from a contested shared mechanic, not raw kills.
Base / goal destruction
MOBA nexus (Dota, LoL) · capture the flag · payload/push
You can lose the fight and still win the game — the objective is the only thing that counts.
Attacker vs. defender
Search & destroy · cargo · one-life asymmetric raid
Roles are usually swapped each round so both sides play both jobs — keeps it fair.
Round elimination
Counter-Strike · Valorant · Rainbow Six Siege · 1v1 sudden death
No respawn within the round — win by wiping the other side or outlasting the clock.
N-gon 3+ independent sides
Multi-way race
Kart racing · season-long placement points
Points scale by finishing rank, not just a binary win — 2nd place still matters.
Multi-side control
3+ team domination/control points · multi-team heist race
Shared contested objective — alliances between sides can shift mid-match. Missing from your original list.
Hidden role / social deduction
Mafia · Werewolf · Among Us
Each role has a different win condition and different information — the asymmetry of knowledge is the game.
Last one standing
Battle royale · extraction royale · shrinking-zone survival
Elimination is permanent for the match — no respawns, no second life.
doesn't fit
the grid at all ↓
Modifier axis Symmetric vs. asymmetric Not a row — a flag on any cell. Duel Role becomes 1-vs-many when sides are uneven (Dead by Daylight, Predator/Juggernaut modes). Same win condition, unequal side sizes.
Outside the grid Sandbox / no win condition Practice range, aim-training lobby, creative/build mode. There's no win condition to plot — every competitive game still needs a zero-stakes space like this.
Parallel to sandbox Progression games Some games aren't won, they're progressed — loot, levels, gear, crafting, economy instead of a win condition. Diablo, Path of Exile, Destiny, Warframe, Monster Hunter.
Meta wrapper Tournament / bracket Not a mode itself — takes any Duel or N-gon cell and repeats it across elimination rounds. Design it as a wrapper layer, not a new cell.
No win condition, but authored Narrative / experience Walking sims, visual novels, story-heavy RPGs. No failure state, just forward progress through authored content. Multiple endings are a branch, not a rank — nothing to leaderboard.
Not one cell — several, stacked Composite / open-world GTA-style games: Solo Objective missions + Sandbox free-roam + Progression (money, gear) + Duel/N-gon Score bolted on for online modes. Big open-world games are rarely one cell.
Third axis, not a column Gameplay modifiers Permadeath, respawn timers, time limits, fog of war, low gravity, shrinking zone, escort, moving objective — these stack onto any cell above, they don't define new ones. "2 sides, Objective, escort payload, respawns, 15-minute limit" is just Duel Objective plus four modifiers — that's Overwatch.
Colosseum · your persistent FFA Live session, not a match Really N-gon Score running as a 24/7 open room instead of discrete matches. Rank with pairwise Glicko-2 rating, not raw kills — each kill is a mini 1v1 result, and the rating-deviation term caps swings until a player has enough games logged. An empty server can't be farmed into #1: killing a low-confidence opponent barely moves your rating.
